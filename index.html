About Us

A market stall that never stopped choosing by hand.

Kieke's Pukies started at a Saturday market stand in 2011, selling only what looked good that morning — nothing more.

One buyer, one relationship at a time

We work with a small, named list of boats and farms — not a distribution network. Our buyers know which boat brought in the halibut and which farm the ribeye came off, because they were there that morning.

Everything is cut, filleted or trimmed after your order comes in, by the same people who chose it. If we wouldn't serve it at our own table, we don't put it in yours.

Ask us where anything came from

Every order comes with the boat or farm named on the packing slip. If you want to know more — the fishing method, the breed, the harvest date — our team can tell you, because they were there.

We publish our full list of partner farms and boats once a season — nothing about it is proprietary.
